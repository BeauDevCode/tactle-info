01

Did Tactle detect my mood?

No. The result first shows facts about the game you played. A separate playful, uncertain feeling question may appear based on those facts. The guess may be wrong; the app cannot determine your emotional or medical state. Some rounds say it is hard to tell.

02

Where is my progress?

Tactle saves up to 100 completed-round summaries and separate best scores on your device. Open Today’s play from the game catalog to see rounds completed today. Replaying one game with the same controls can show first and latest saved facts; tap a round to open its details. Earlier rounds may have rolled off the 100-round history, and older saved rounds without dates do not appear in Today. The game has no account or cloud sync. If local saving fails, the result screen tells you that progress might not have been saved.

03

How do I clear saved game data?

Open About Tactle → Clear saved game history in the app, then confirm. That clears local round summaries and best scores. If the app reports that clearing failed, try again or email us for help. Device backups may retain an older copy.

04

Can I play offline?

Yes. The ten games work offline. Opening a web page or sending an email uses your browser or email service and requires a connection.
