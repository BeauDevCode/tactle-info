Tactle is a short-game collection for iPhone. This policy explains the data the app uses, what stays on your device, and how to clear it.

Last updated November 6, 2026

◎Game data stays on your device.

Tactle does not run a game account, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking service.

01

What the app uses

When you finish a game, Tactle creates an aggregate round summary: the game played, round duration, actions, successes, misses, retries, explorations, score, whether guided timing controls were used, and a UTC completion time for newly recorded rounds. Older saved rounds do not have a known completion date. Tactle does not save a raw touch trail or pointer positions.

The result uses game-specific counts to show what happened. Once three earlier dated rounds of the same game and control mode exist, it can show one named game measure beside the median of those earlier rounds. The Today’s play screen counts completed rounds, games, and round time for your device’s local calendar day and lists those rounds. It can compare first and latest facts from repeated rounds of the same game and control mode, and you can expand a saved round to see its facts and any available median. Today uses only the last 100 saved summaries; older rounds may have rolled off, and undated rounds are excluded.

A separate playful, tentative feeling question may appear, or “Hard to tell from this round.” Gameplay cannot establish how you actually feel, your personality, or a mental-health condition. The question is not a health measurement, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency service. Tactle does not save a separate emotional profile.

02

What is saved

The last 100 completed-round summaries are stored in local app storage on your device. Best scores are stored separately, so a best score can remain after its older round summary has been trimmed. If damaged saved history cannot be fully read, Tactle keeps a local recovery notice so Today’s play can tell you that some records were lost, even after another round is saved. If saving fails, the app can still show the just-finished result and tells you that progress might not have been saved.

You can play without creating an account or giving the app your name, email address, or birth date. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, or biometric access.

03

Where information goes

Gameplay works offline. Tactle has no app-operated backend, cloud sync, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or third-party tracking. The app does not transmit your game summaries or feeling prompts to us.

If you open this policy or the support page from the app, your browser makes a normal request to this GitHub Pages site. These pages do not add analytics scripts, forms, advertising, or third-party fonts. GitHub may process request information to serve the pages. If you choose to email support, your message and email address are handled through your email service and ours so we can respond to your request; email is separate from gameplay.

Support messages remain in our email inbox until deleted; there is no automatic deletion schedule. You can email us to request deletion of correspondence we control. We will remove those messages from our active inbox, subject to any legal obligation to keep them. Copies may remain in your own email, email-provider systems, or backups.

04

How to clear local data

In Tactle, open About Tactle → Clear saved game history and confirm. This removes stored round summaries, saved best scores, and any local recovery notice from that device. If removal fails, the app says it could not fully clear the data, and you can retry.

Removing the app generally removes its local storage from that device. A device backup may retain an older copy according to your device and backup settings. Tactle has no developer-held game account or server history to delete.

05

Changes and questions

We may update this policy as the app changes, and we will update the date above. For privacy questions or help with local data, email chillibeef123@gmail.com or visit the support page.
